# Provenance

This public module is a reduced form of the evidence/review boundary used in my private research and acquisition tooling.

## Preserved

- evidence grouped by independent source;
- proposal serialization;
- content-bound approval digest;
- approval expiry;
- one-use authorization for irreversible actions.

## Rewritten for public review

Discovery connectors, browser sessions, private prospect data, email accounts, and external-action transport are intentionally absent.

## Claim boundary

The repository proves the approval semantics, not that any outreach, purchase, submission, or business outcome occurred.
